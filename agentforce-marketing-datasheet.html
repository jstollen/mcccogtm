Bridge+ Editions

Connect existing MCE / MCAE setups to the MC Next platform layer

Engagement+

MCE → MCN Interop

Layers MCN agentic campaign creation and Conversational Email on existing MCE automation setups. Journey Builder tenant remains fully operational.

Account Engagement+

MCAE → MCN Interop

Adds MCN next-gen campaign and agentic capabilities alongside existing MCAE engagement workflows and CRM lead matching.

Personalization+

Personalization → Agentforce

Layers Agentforce-driven orchestration parameters over base Salesforce Personalization decision matrices. Existing MCP customers get this at no additional cost.

Intelligence+

Intelligence → Core AI Layer

Adds automated AI summaries and calculated paid media optimization points over core cross-channel reporting files. Includes legacy MCI access.
